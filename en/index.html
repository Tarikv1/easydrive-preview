Who can get a German licence?

You need to be a resident of Germany (your main home, at least 185 days a year). If you live in Weil am Rhein, Lörrach or the surrounding area and work or study in Basel, you are in the right place. Residents of Switzerland cannot take a German licence – please contact the cantonal office instead.

Moved to Germany with a licence from outside the EU? Depending on your country you may need to retake the test – we check your case for free.

Car, automatic, trailer

Three licence classes, one goal: pass safely and without stress – at your pace.

Class B – car

The standard car licence, from age 17 (accompanied driving) or 18. 14 theory lessons, 12 special drives (country road, motorway, night), computer theory test available in English.

Class B197 – automatic without restriction

Learn and take the test in an automatic, add 10 lessons in a manual car and a 15-minute check drive – and you may drive both. No "automatic only" code in your licence.

Class BE – trailer

Trailers above 750 kg, up to 3.5 t. No theory test, 5 special drives, practical test with trailer.

Five steps to your licence

  1. Sign up

    Come by, call or WhatsApp us. Free advice in English.

  2. Apply

    Licence application at the Landratsamt Lörrach driving-licence office (appointment needed): eyesight test, first-aid course, biometric photo, ID. We help you fill it in.

  3. Theory

    12 + 2 double lessons for class B. Learning app and theory test in English.

  4. Practice

    Driving lessons at your pace – automatic or manual – plus the 12 special drives.

  5. Test

    Theory test, then the practical test with the TÜV SÜD examiner (instructions in German – we train you for them).

Licence in 21 days*

Once you have passed the theory test, we schedule daily driving lessons and bring you to the practical test within three weeks. Honest planning: if 21 days is not realistic for you, we say so and plan individually.
