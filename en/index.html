Frequently asked questions

When can I start learning to drive?

You can start training for class B half a year before your 17th birthday (accompanied driving from 17, BF17), or at 17½ for a driving licence from 18. You can take the theory test no earlier than three months before your birthday, and the practical test no earlier than one month before it.

How long does it take to get a driving licence with you?

That depends on the time you have. At a normal pace, allow three to five months. With our intensive course, you can complete the practical training and test in 21 days – provided you have passed the theory test.

What is the difference between class B and B197?

With B197, you learn in an automatic car and also take the test in an automatic. You also complete at least ten driving lessons in a manual car, followed by a 15-minute test drive with your driving instructor. This means you have no automatic-only restriction (code 78) and can drive both.

Which documents do I need for the application?

An identity card or passport, a biometric passport photo, an eye test (no more than two years old), proof of completing a first-aid course (9 teaching units) and confirmation of your driving school registration. For BF17, you also need details of your accompanying drivers. The licensing office at the Lörrach district office (Landratsamt) offers appointments online.

Can I take theory and driving lessons in Turkish, Kurdish or English?

Yes. Our team teaches in German, English, Turkish and Kurdish. You can take the theory test in German, English or Turkish, among other languages; Kurdish is not available for the test, but we prepare you in Kurdish.

Can I switch to you from another driving school?

Yes, at any time. You just need a training record from your previous driving school. Theory and driving lessons you have already completed count towards your training.

I live in Basel – can I get my driving licence with you?

You can only get a German driving licence if you are normally resident in Germany (at least 185 days a year). If you live in Weil am Rhein, Lörrach or the surrounding area and work or study in Basel, you are in the right place.
